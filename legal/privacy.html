中文

AI模型領航員是非官方、獨立開發的 AI 模型決策工作台，提供模型比較、API 成本試算、離線 Token 粗略估算與離線模型資料庫，與 OpenAI、Anthropic、Google、Meta、xAI 等公司均無關聯。

  • 無帳號與行為追蹤。App 不要求註冊、不建立帳號、不追蹤您的身分，也沒有分析、廣告或第三方追蹤 SDK。
  • 公開動態與離線工具：模型資料庫與成本試算可在裝置上使用。AI 動態透過 HTTPS 讀取 GitHub Pages 上的公開摘要，並保存最近成功載入的資料供離線閱讀。請求不附帶您的專案、備註或 Token 輸入；網站主機仍會接收連線所需的 IP 位址與 HTTP 請求資訊，並依其政策處理。App 沒有帳號或自建雲端同步。
  • 工作台資料：您建立的專案名稱、用途、必要條件、模型選擇、用量、成本參數、採用決策與備註，皆只儲存在您的裝置上，不會傳送給我們。
  • Token 長度估算：輸入文字只在裝置上以模型中立的近似規則處理，不會上傳。估算不是任何特定模型 tokenizer 的精確結果。
  • 對外連結：當您主動點選模型或新聞的資料來源連結時，App 會以系統瀏覽器元件開啟該網址；該網站將依其自身隱私政策處理您的連線。我們不記錄您點了哪些連結。
  • 匯出：您可主動透過系統分享功能匯出 Markdown 摘要。匯出目的地由您選擇，我們不接觸匯出內容。
  • 付費：App 所有功能皆免費，無訂閱、無 App 內購買，我們不接觸任何付款資訊。
  • 本機偏好：語言等設定只存在您的裝置上；刪除 App 會移除裝置上的 App 資料；您自行匯出的檔案及作業系統備份須另行管理。
  • 兒童：本 App 不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，且不會蒐集兒童個人資料。
  • 政策變更：若日後有變更，將更新本頁最後更新日期。

English

AI Model Navigator is an unofficial, independently developed AI model decision workspace for model comparison, API cost planning, offline rough token estimates, and an offline model database. It is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, xAI, or any other company.

  • No accounts or behavioral tracking. No registration, no accounts, no identity tracking, and no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Public updates and offline tools: the model database and cost calculations run on your device. AI Updates reads public summaries from GitHub Pages over HTTPS and stores the last successful response for offline reading. Requests do not include your projects, notes, or token input. The hosting service receives connection information such as your IP address and HTTP request details and processes it under its own policy. The app has no accounts or custom cloud sync.
  • Workspace data: project names, use cases, requirements, selected models, usage and cost assumptions, adoption decisions, and notes are stored on your device only and are never sent to us.
  • Token length estimates: input text is processed only on your device with a model-neutral approximation and is never uploaded. It is not an exact tokenizer result for any specific model.
  • Outbound links: when you tap a model or news source link, the app opens that URL in the system browser component; that site handles your connection under its own privacy policy. We do not log which links you open.
  • Export: you can export a Markdown summary through the system share sheet. You choose the destination; we never see the exported content.
  • Payments: every feature is free. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase, and we never handle payment information.
  • Local preferences such as language are stored only on your device; deleting the app removes its on-device data; exported files and operating-system backups must be managed separately.
  • Children: this app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect children's personal data.
  • Changes: any future change will be reflected in the "last updated" date above.
